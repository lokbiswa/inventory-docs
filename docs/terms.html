The app

Inventory Promos is a private Clover Register app for this merchant. It is not a public Clover App Market product. By installing or using the app on this merchant’s Clover devices, you agree to these terms.

What the app does

The app:

  • Reads Clover inventory (items and tags) to determine promotion eligibility.
  • Reads and updates open orders only, adding or removing line-item discounts named Promo: {name}.
  • Stores promotion rules on the device and on the merchant’s inventory server.

The app does not collect customer card data. It does not sell data. It does not take payments or change closed (paid or locked) orders.
