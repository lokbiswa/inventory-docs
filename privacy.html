Who this applies to

This policy describes how the Inventory Promos Clover Register app (“the app”) handles information. The app is a private app for this merchant. It is not a public Clover App Market product and is not offered to other merchants.

What the app accesses

On Clover devices where it is installed, the app:

  • Reads Clover inventory (items and tags) so it can tell which items are eligible for a promotion.
  • Reads and updates open orders only, to add or remove line-item discounts named Promo: {name} (for example, Promo: any 2 Red Bull for $5).

The app does not change paid, locked, or otherwise closed orders. It does not process payments.

What the app does not collect

  • It does not collect customer card data, CVV, PIN, or other payment-card details.
  • It does not sell data, and it does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • These pages do not use cookies, analytics, or other tracking.

Where information is stored

Promotion rules are stored on the Clover register (on the device) and on the merchant’s inventory server. The inventory server is operated for this merchant to create and push promotions. The app receives those rules so the register can apply them.

Order and inventory records remain in the merchant’s Clover account. The app uses them only to apply or remove the promo discounts described above.

Sharing

Information the app uses stays with this merchant’s Clover account and inventory server, except where Clover itself stores or processes merchant data as part of providing the Clover platform. We do not sell that information.
